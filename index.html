Water damage emergency

Water damage? Do this first

Every minute counts. These steps protect your family and your home while help is on the way.

  1. Safety firstGet everyone out of the wet area. Don't walk through standing water if electricity may be nearby, and stay away from outlets, wiring and the electrical panel. If a ceiling is sagging with water, stay out.
  2. Shut off the main waterFind the main valve where the line enters your home: basement, utility room, crawlspace, near the water meter, or an outdoor box. A round handle turns clockwise until it stops. A lever handle turns a quarter turn so it sits crosswise to the pipe. Can't find it or it won't turn? Call your water utility.
  3. Drain the linesWith the main off, open a low faucet and a high faucet so the water left in the pipes drains out and the pressure drops.
  4. Cut the power, only if it's safeIf the panel is dry and you can reach it without stepping in water, switch off the breakers for the wet area. If the panel is wet or water is near it, don't touch it. Call your utility or an electrician. If you smell gas or see sparks, leave and call 911.
  5. Call us nowCall or text (203) 491-4779. The sooner the water is out and the area is drying, the less damage and the lower the mold risk. Mold can start in 24 to 48 hours.
  6. Take photos, call your insuranceShoot video and photos of the damage and your belongings before you move anything. Tell your insurance company as soon as you can.
  7. Save what you safely canLift furniture off wet carpet, pull up small rugs and move valuables and electronics somewhere dry. Open windows for air if the weather allows.

Don't do this

  • Don't use a household vacuum on water.
  • Don't turn on appliances or ceiling lights while standing in water or under a wet ceiling.
  • Don't wait to see if it dries on its own.
  • Don't paint or cover over mold. It has to be cleaned and dried first.
  • With sewage or floodwater, avoid contact and wash your hands well.
Our process

Step by step, for every service

Know exactly what to expect before we arrive.

We leave your crawlspace clean and looking new, whether the floor is dirt or concrete.

  1. Walk-through and photosWe check the whole space for standing water, moisture and damage, and show you what we find before we start.
  2. Clear it outStored items, debris, old plastic and leftover material are bagged and hauled away.
  3. Clean floor and wallsDirt or concrete, we clean the surfaces so the liner lays flat on a clean base.
  4. Check for moistureIf we see leaks, standing water or mold, we tell you and fix it or recommend the fix before covering anything.
  5. Lay the white linerA heavy-duty white vapor barrier goes down with seams overlapped and taped, and runs up the walls and piers.
  6. Wrap piers, final walk-throughWe wrap the piers, seal the edges and walk the space with you.

We wrap your HVAC ducts to help stop condensation and energy loss.

  1. Inspect the ductsWe check supply and return ducts and any existing insulation for gaps, tears and loose sections. If there's moisture or mold, we deal with it before wrapping.
  2. Clean and sealWe wipe the surface and seal seams and joints with foil tape or mastic so air can't leak out.
  3. Wrap with insulationReflective insulation goes around the duct with the foil side out, snug but not crushed, and overlapped at the seams.
  4. Tape and secureSeams and ends are taped and held in place so nothing sags or comes loose.
  5. Final checkWe go over every run, tidy up and show you the finished job.

We remove the mold and deal with the cause so it doesn't come back.

  1. Inspect and find the moistureWe size up the problem and find where the moisture is coming from. If the source isn't fixed, mold returns.
  2. Contain the areaWe seal the work area with plastic sheeting and cover vents so spores don't spread through the rest of the house.
  3. Protect the crewRespirators, gloves, eye protection and disposable suits.
  4. Remove what can't be savedMoldy porous materials such as drywall or insulation are cut out, double-bagged and hauled away.
  5. Clean and dryHEPA vacuum, damp-wipe the surfaces and dry everything. We never paint or cover over mold.
  6. Final checkWe confirm no visible mold or musty smell remains, and tell you how to keep the area dry.

We move fast, get the water out and dry the area to stop damage and mold. If it's happening now, see the emergency steps above first.

  1. Respond fastWe check safety, find the source and stop it if water is still running.
  2. Remove standing waterStanding water comes out with pumps and wet vacuums.
  3. Remove what's too wet to saveSoaked carpet and pad, wet insulation and damaged drywall come out so the area can dry.
  4. Dry it outFans and dehumidifiers run until moisture readings come down. Drying within 24 to 48 hours helps prevent mold.
  5. Clean and sanitizeWe clean the surfaces and the materials that can be saved.
  6. Final check and paperworkWe check for signs of mold and give you photos and details of the work for your insurance company.

This is general safety guidance. Every job is different, so we'll go over what applies to your home in your estimate.
